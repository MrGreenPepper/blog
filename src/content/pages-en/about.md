Hello! My name is Sebastian Trümper—but most of the thoughts in my head don’t have a name.

They just pop up. While I’m drinking coffee, looking at a data set, or in the middle of the night. Why is this computer behaving this way? What would an economist say about this everyday phenomenon? And is what we all take for granted actually that obvious? My mind likes to jump around—between data and philosophy, between economics and world events. And yes, sometimes it just wanders to the weather. You’ve got to land somewhere.

I feel particularly at home where things get a little offbeat: with thought experiments that sound absurd at first glance but suddenly don’t at all upon closer inspection. And I’ll openly admit it—when everyone in a group nods in agreement, my fingers start to itch. That’s when I play devil’s advocate—not out of a desire to argue, but out of conviction: an idea that hasn’t faced any opposition hasn’t proven anything yet.

This blog is my notebook with the door wide open. No overarching theme, no set agenda—just genuine curiosity. Sometimes it’s technical, sometimes philosophical, sometimes economic, sometimes all at once. I don’t promise any ready-made answers. But I do promise to take the questions seriously—especially the uncomfortable ones.

So if you feel like joining in the discussion, challenging the status quo, or just watching someone think out loud: Welcome. Feel free to raise an eyebrow. That’s exactly what this is for.
